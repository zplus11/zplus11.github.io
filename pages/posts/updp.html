THE universal property of direct products

22 May, 2026



I studied module theory the past semester, and noticeably I couldn’t find the “complete version” universal property of direct products anywhere I’ve looked so far. That is the version we studied in this course. I am thus going to write it out here.

Theorem (Universal property of direct product). Let R be a ring. Let M be a (left-) R module and {Mα}α ∈ J be a family R modules. Then, M ≅ ∏αMα if and only if there exists a family or R-homs {pα : M → Mα} satisfying the following property: for any R-module A and any associated family of R-homs {fα : A → Mα}, there exists a unique R-hom ϕ : A → M such that fα = ϕpα for each α ∈ J.

Proof. We first prove the forward part. Let ρ : M → ∏αMα be an isomorphism. We prove existence of the required family of R-homs {pα}. Let πβ : ∏αMα → Mβ be the canonical projections. We propose that pα = πα ∘ ρ satisfy the given property.

To show that this proposed family {pα} satisfies the given property, we let A be any other R-module and {fα : A → Mα} be an associated family of R-homs.

We produce the required ϕ : A → M. First consider η : A → ∏αMα defined by

η(a) := (fα(a))α.

Since each fα is an R-hom therefore so is η. Then define ϕ = ρ−1 ∘ η.
